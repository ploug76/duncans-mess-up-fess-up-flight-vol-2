# Duncan's Mess Up Fess Up Flight 2: Wings of Grace

Volume 2 of [Duncan's Mess Up Fess Up Flight](https://ploug76.github.io/duncans-mess-up-fess-up-flight/) (Volume 1 is unchanged). A Book of Heaven Studios game.

**Play:** https://ploug76.github.io/duncans-mess-up-fess-up-flight-vol-2/

- **Quest:** fly Duncan the angel through 5 painted worlds (Courthouse Clouds, Commandment Canyon, Town of Red Tape, Storm of Guilt, The Pearly Gates), 30 stages with a boss at the end of every world. Catch the doves of grace, find the lost pages of the Book of Heaven. 3 stars per stage, saved progress.
- **Mess up? Fess up!** Bump into a law and Duncan messes up; tap FESS UP and he's forgiven and flies on.
- **Endless Grace** mode with a saved best.
- Story intro and ending, with a big red SKIP on every page.
- **Controls:** tap anywhere to flap (little taps, little hops). Keyboard (Space, Up or W) and game controllers (USB/Bluetooth, A to flap, Start to pause) fully supported, menus included. No on-screen D-pad.
- Works offline (PWA) and updates itself on open.
